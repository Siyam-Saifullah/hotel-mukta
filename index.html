Welcome to Hotel Mukta

Your Serene Sanctuary in Vibrant Rajshahi

Hotel Mukta International stands proudly in the historic and bustling hub of Rajshahi. Whether you are traveling for business, university engagements, or exploring the iconic Padma River bank and Rajshahi silk heritage, our property offers prime accessibility with quiet comfort.
